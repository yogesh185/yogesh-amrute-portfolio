- `about-portrait.webp` — real workspace/editorial portrait.

- `music-keyboard.webp` — authentic keyboard image.

- `aadi-desktop.webp` / `aadi-mobile.webp` — actual project screenshots.
- `yarvo-desktop.webp` / `yarvo-mobile.webp` — actual agency screenshots.
